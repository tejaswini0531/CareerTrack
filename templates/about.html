ABOUT CAREERTRACK

Making Your Job Search Organized and Simple

CareerTrack is a job application tracking system designed to help job seekers organize their applications, manage interviews and monitor their career progress in one place.

🎯

Our Purpose

To provide a simple and organized platform where users can manage their job search activities without losing track of opportunities.

🚀

Our Objective

To make job application tracking easier by providing application management, status updates, interview tracking and progress statistics.

WHY CAREERTRACK?

Designed for Your Career Journey

✓ Manage multiple job applications
✓ Update application statuses
✓ Track interview schedules
✓ Search and filter applications
✓ View application statistics
✓ Secure user authentication
